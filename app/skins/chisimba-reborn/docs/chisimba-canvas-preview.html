Welcome to Chisimba 26

Chisimba 26 represents the modern evolution of the AVOIR project's landmark software framework. Built specifically for training enterprises, Chisimba 26 harnesses responsible AI to drive efficiency and excellence. From automating assessment administration to personalising student learning pathways, our platform optimises training operations while putting instructors back at the heart of the learning experience.
